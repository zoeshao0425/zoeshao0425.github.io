abstract: >-
  Top-k expert routing in Mixture-of-Experts models is discrete and non-differentiable. ProbMoE
  treats expert selection as a distribution over size-constrained expert subsets and performs
  routing as probabilistic inference, giving tractable gradients and adaptive per-token budgets.
