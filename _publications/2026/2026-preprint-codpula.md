abstract: >-
  Codpula jointly trains a diffusion language model and a tractable probabilistic inference layer
  to capture context-dependent relationships among tokens predicted in parallel. Exact conditional
  queries guide which tokens to commit together, improving accuracy-latency trade-offs in mathematical
  reasoning and code generation.
