abstract: >-
  Adding latent states to probabilistic circuits yields diminishing likelihood gains. This work
  uses mutual information and state-usage measurements to study how training uses that capacity.
  Early measurements guide initialization and tree selection, improving final performance across
  the tested latent sizes.
